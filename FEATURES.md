# Detailed Features Documentation

## 1. Income Tax (ITR) Module

### ITR Calculator
- **Purpose**: Help users calculate their income tax liability
- **Features**:
  - Salary income input
  - Business income tracking
  - Capital gains
  - Other income sources
  - Deduction calculator (Section 80C, 80D, 80E, 80G)
  - Tax slab calculation
  - Surcharge & Cess calculation
  - Refund estimation

### ITR Form Filler
- **Purpose**: Guide users through ITR filing
- **Features**:
  - Step-by-step form wizard
  - Real-time validation
  - Auto-population from records
  - Error checking
  - PDF generation
  - E-filing integration

---

## 2. GST Module

### GST Registration
- **Purpose**: Help users understand and register for GST
- **Features**:
  - Eligibility checker
  - Required documents list
  - Step-by-step registration guide
  - Timeline tracker
  - Registration status updater

### GST Return Filing
- **Purpose**: Simplify GST return filing
- **Features**:
  - GSTR-1, GSTR-3B templates
  - Invoice auto-sync
  - Debit/Credit note tracking
  - ITC (Input Tax Credit) management
  - Payment tracking
  - File history

### GST Payments
- **Purpose**: Calculate and track GST payments
- **Features**:
  - Monthly/Quarterly calculations
  - Payment due date reminders
  - Payment history
  - Late payment penalty calculator

---
